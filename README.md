# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard – Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes the **Sample Superstore** dataset to give a quick, clear view of sales performance across regions, customer segments, and product categories, including a sales forecast and a key-drivers analysis.

---

## 🎯 Project Goal

Help business owners and managers answer questions like:

- How much are we selling, and how profitable are we?
- Which regions and product categories drive the most sales?
- How do sales break down by region → customer segment → category?
- How are sales trending over time, and what can we expect next?
- Which factors influence sales the most?

---

---

## 🧩 Dashboard Components

| Visual | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Card visuals** | Display the main KPIs at a glance |
| **Decomposition Tree** | Break down sales step by step: Region → Segment → Category |
| **Bar Chart** | Compare sales across regions |
| **Donut Chart** | Show each category's share of total sales |
| **Line / Area Chart + Forecast** | Track sales over Year, Quarter, Month and Day, with a future forecast |
| **Slicer** | Filter the whole report by product category |
| **Key Influencers** | Identify the factors that most affect sales |

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **West** and **East** are the top-performing regions; **South** has the lowest sales.
- **Technology** is the best-selling category (**36.4%**), followed by **Furniture** (**32.29%**) and **Office Supplies** (**31.3%**).
- Sales show a clear **seasonal pattern** across the years, and the forecast extends the trend beyond 2018.
- The Key Influencers visual highlights how profit and category relate to increases in sales.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download the `.pbix` file from this repository.
2. Open it with **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the **Category slicer** and click on any visual to filter the whole report.
4. Use the **Decomposition Tree** to drill down into sales by region, segment and category.
